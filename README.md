# Open Access – Africa

**Open Access** is a public archive that maps the laws colonial powers used to legalize extraction, exploitation, cultural suppression and the resulting legal actions taken in modern society to demand redress or monetary compensation for systematic oppression. Volunteers research and document four connected tracks:

| Track                        | What it documents                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Colonial Laws**            | The legal instruments colonial powers used to legalize extraction, exploitation, and cultural suppression |
| **Reparations Cases**        | What justice has looked like after systematic colonial legislation. Delivered or refused                  |
| **Modern Cases**             | Harms happening today, and documented accountability                                                      |
| **Modern Laws and Policies** | Present-day laws and policies, national and international, that either enable harm or demand redress      |

Alongside the archive, we are also mapping apprenticeship networks that preserve vocational careers and heritage knowledge. But this is an on-the-ground activity involving interviews, translation, and local input of respective communities. Message in Discord if you are interested in supporting.

### Researcher

Researchers document colonial laws, reparations cases, modern cases, and modern laws and policies.

#### Setup

- [ ] Signed up with the [Volunteer with OA](https://airtable.com/appbrLESyzc7qVxYM/pagrn5dZfLdrbW7z0/form) form
- [ ] Joined Discord
- [ ] Airtable account (if a seat is available; otherwise you can work entirely from the forms or [Volunteer Portal](https://airtable.com/appbrLESyzc7qVxYM/pagz70y0jPUvSW4Ec). )
- [ ] Optional: Obsidian, to read this guide offline and organize thoughts (Use the Relay plugin for collaborating with team)

No downloads are required. Everything works in a web browser.

#### Your workflow

1. **Get assigned.** An admin assigns you a **Research Task** and a batch of 5 **Research Leads**.
2. **Read the brief.** Open your task in the [Volunteer Portal](https://airtable.com/appbrLESyzc7qVxYM/pagz70y0jPUvSW4Ec). Read the instructions and the linked **Resources**, especially the worked example.
3. **Research each lead.** Start from the notes on the lead and the starting source lists.
4. **Submit.** Use the form for your track (see [Quick links](#quick-links)), with **the same email you signed up with**.
5. **Tell us.** Post in Discord when you've submitted, and ask for your next batch.

> [!NOTE]
> **Email for submissions**
> Within the Airtable form, an email is required. You may create a real dummy email, or use a fake email. Just make sure it is the same for each of your submissions.

#### Where to find things in Airtable

| Table          | What's there                                       |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Research Tasks | Your task brief, instructions, and deadline        |
| Research Leads | The specific laws, cases, or speeches to research  |
| Resources      | Guides, worked examples, and starting source lists |
#### What the statuses mean
| Task Status | Meaning                                    |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Unassigned  | Nobody is working on it yet                |
| Assigned    | A volunteer has it                         |
| Submitted   | Entry received, waiting for review         |
| In Review   | QA reviewer is checking it                 |
| Published   | Live on the public archive (soon website!) |
#### Quality standards

Every entry needs:

- [ ] **2 or more credible sources**: academic articles, court or government records, archives, reputable news
- [ ] **A full write-up**:
  - Colonial Laws: a 2–3 sentence **Summary** and a **200+ word Analysis**
  - Reparations Cases: a **150+ word Case Summary**
  - Modern Cases and Modern Laws and Policies: a 2–3 sentence **Summary** and a **150+ word Analysis**
- [ ] **Every required field** completed
- [ ] **Your signup email** in the email field

**Sources without a link.** Books, archive files, and gazettes often have no URL. Put the full citation in **Source Text**: author, title, publisher, year, page or archive reference. This is a required field so if you only have one link and no text, you can prioritize the **Source Text** field.

**Speeches.** Speeches are recorded as **sources on the related law**, not as separate entries. Include the speaker, date, venue, and where the transcript can be found.

**Quotes.** Quote only what you've verified in the original source. If you can't confirm the exact wording, paraphrase and cite.

Each track also has its own outcome status, separate from our research progress:

- **Reparations Status** (Reparations Cases): Proposed, Ongoing, Partially implemented, Completed, Rejected
- **Case Status** (Modern Cases): the state of the accountability effort, e.g. Filed, Ongoing, Settled, Dismissed
- **Current Status** (Modern Laws and Policies): Proposed, In force, Amended, Challenged, Repealed or revoked

#### FAQ

**My submission shows "Email Not Matched."**
You probably used a different email from your sign-up, or your account isn't approved yet. Tell an admin in Discord.

**I found a law or case that isn't on my lead list.**
Great. Post it in Discord so an admin can add it as a lead, assign it to you, then research it.

**I'm stuck.**
Ask in Discord. Someone probably has the answer or message me.

### Professional

Professionals bring specialization, such as law, architecture, or archival work. You research like a [Researcher](#researcher), and your expertise helps make the archive credible.

#### Setup

- [ ] Signed up with the [Volunteer with OA](https://airtable.com/appbrLESyzc7qVxYM/pagrn5dZfLdrbW7z0/form) form, with your field in **Skills / Background**
- [ ] Joined Discord
- [ ] Airtable account (if a seat is available)

#### What you do

**1. Research.** Follow the same workflow as a [Researcher](#researcher): take an assigned task and leads, research each one, and submit through the forms with your signup email.

**2. Advise on accuracy.** Where your expertise matters most:

| Field                                               | What to check                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Legal Mechanism for Redress** (Reparations Cases) | By what legal route was the remedy delivered? *For further thinking: does the classification represent the outcomes?* |
| **Reparations Status**                              | Is the outcome described correctly: Proposed, Ongoing, Partially implemented, Completed, or Rejected?                 |
| **Instrument Type** and **Summary** (Colonial Laws) | Is the legal form right and does the summary reflect the law's actual effect?                                         |
| **Mechanism of Harm** (all research tracks)         | Do the tags match the harm described, and does the write-up support them?                                             |
| **Sources**                                         | Are primary legal sources (statutes, gazettes, judgments) cited where possible?                                       |

**3. Help the team.**
- Answer legal or technical questions from researchers in Discord (optional)
- Suggest new leads: laws, cases, or precedents we've missed
- Flag entries that make legal claims the sources don't support

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Not legal advice.** Your contributions support historical and educational research. Open Access doesn't provide legal advice, and entries shouldn't be presented as such.

#### Standards

The same standards as a [Researcher](#researcher) apply: 2+ credible sources, full write-ups, and your signup email on every submission. Where possible, cite the **primary legal source** (the statute, ordinance, or judgment itself) alongside secondary analysis.

### QA Reviewer

QA reviewers are the last check before an entry goes public. You make sure every published record is accurate, well sourced, and complete.

#### Setup

- [ ] Signed up with the [Volunteer with OA](https://airtable.com/appbrLESyzc7qVxYM/pagrn5dZfLdrbW7z0/form) form
- [ ] Joined Discord
- [ ] **Airtable account (required)**: you'll change statuses and link records

#### Your review queue

In each research table, look for records where **Task Status** is **Submitted**.

1. Change **Task Status** to **In Review** so others know you've picked it up.
2. Work through the checklist below.
3. Then either:
   - **Approve:** set **Task Status** to **Published** and add yourself to **Verified By**, or
   - **Send back:** set **Task Status** to **Assigned**, and leave a comment on the record (and in Discord) explaining what needs fixing.

#### Review checklist

**Every entry**
- [ ] **Email Not Matched** is *not* ticked. If it is, confirm with an admin who submitted it before approving.
- [ ] All required fields are complete
- [ ] **2+ credible sources**, across the Source URLs and **Source Text**
- [ ] Source links work, and they actually support the claims made
- [ ] No unverified quotes: quoted wording matches the original source
- [ ] Neutral, factual tone

**Colonial Laws**
- [ ] **Summary** is 2–3 sentences
- [ ] **Analysis** is 200+ words and explains the **Mechanism of Harm**
- [ ] **Year Enacted**, **Colonial Power**, and **Colony / Territory** are consistent with the sources
- [ ] **Estimated Value Extracted** states its currency and basis, or is left blank

**Reparations Cases**
- [ ] **Case Summary** is 150+ words
- [ ] **Outcome Type**, **Legal Mechanism**, and **Reparations Status** match the sources
- [ ] Amounts include the currency and are sourced

**Apprenticeship Opportunities**
- [ ] The organization is real and the contact details look valid
- [ ] **How to Apply** is clear
- [ ] Nothing in the listing is misleading about cost, stipend, or duration

#### After you publish

- In **Research Leads**, find the lead this entry came from: set **Lead Status** to **Done** and link the published record in its **Resulting…** field.
- Where relevant, link related records, such as **Related Colonial Laws** on a case, or **Related Reparations Cases** on a law.
- For apprenticeships, fill in **Verified Date**.

#### Tips

- **Don't review your own submissions.** Ask another reviewer.
- If a legal claim looks shaky, ask a [Professional](#professional) in Discord.
- When sending something back, be specific and kind. Most volunteers are learning.

### Data Entry

Data entry volunteers build the **Apprenticeship Marketplace**: listings for apprenticeships that preserve craft and heritage knowledge, from weaving to repair trades.

#### Setup

- [ ] Signed up with the [Volunteer with OA](https://airtable.com/appbrLESyzc7qVxYM/pagrn5dZfLdrbW7z0/form) form
- [ ] Joined Discord
- [ ] Airtable account (if a seat is available)

#### Your workflow

1. **Get your list.** An admin assigns you organizations to contact, usually 3 at a time.
2. **Reach out.** Contact each organization and explain Open Access (a short script is below).
3. **Gather the details** listed in the checklist.
4. **Submit** each opportunity with the [Submit Apprenticeship Opportunity](https://airtable.com/appbrLESyzc7qVxYM/pag1EnfYj2JYZc6Ia/form) form.
5. **Post in Discord** when you've submitted.

#### Details to collect

**The opportunity**
- [ ] Opportunity name
- [ ] Craft / skill
- [ ] Location and country
- [ ] Description
- [ ] Duration
- [ ] Apprentice level (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced)
- [ ] Cost or stipend
- [ ] How to apply

**Focus**
- [ ] Does it involve technology? (details)
- [ ] Does it have a heritage focus? (details)

**Organization**
- [ ] Organization name, location, and website
- [ ] A short "about" description
- [ ] Logo and photos, **with permission to use them**

**Contact**
- [ ] Contact person, email, and phone

#### Outreach script (English)

> Hello, my name is [your name] and I volunteer with **Open Access**, a project building a public directory of apprenticeships that preserve craft and heritage knowledge.
>
> We want to include [organization]'s apprenticeship in our marketplace so more people can find it. Could you share a few details, such as what the apprenticeship covers, how long it lasts, and how people can apply?
>
> Listing is free, and we'll share the entry with you before it goes live.

#### Standards

- Only list details the organization has **confirmed**.
- Get **permission** before submitting photos or logos.
- The contact details are the organization's public contact, not a private individual's personal number, unless they've agreed.

### Admin

Admins keep Open Access running: approving volunteers, assigning work, maintaining Airtable, and keeping this guide current.

#### Setup

- [ ] Discord, with admin permissions in the server
- [ ] Airtable, as a base collaborator with **Creator** access
- [ ] Obsidian Desktop, with the plugins in [Obsidian setup](#obsidian-setup)
- [ ] Access to the Zapier account (temporary, until the website takes over notifications)

#### Core responsibilities

**1. Onboard volunteers.** For each new sign-up:
- Book an onboarding call
- Set **Status** to **Active**, fill in **Join Date**, confirm **Role**
- Assign a first task and about 5 leads
- Send the welcome email

**2. Assign work.**
- **Research Tasks:** add volunteers to **Assigned To**
- **Research Leads:** set **Claimed By** and **Lead Status** to **Claimed**
- Add new leads when volunteers suggest them

**3. Watch the queues.**
- New submissions (**Task Status** is **Submitted**) are picked up by [QA Reviewers](#qa-reviewer)
- Records with **Email Not Matched** ticked: confirm who submitted them
- Volunteers with **Status** set to **Onboarding** are waiting for approval

**4. Manage Airtable seats.**
- The free plan allows **5 collaborators**, including the owner
- When someone leaves, remove their access to free the seat
- Volunteers without a seat can still work entirely through the forms

#### Weekly routine

- [ ] **Monday:** review sign-ups waiting in Onboarding; approve or schedule calls
- [ ] **Monday:** check each volunteer has enough leads for the week
- [ ] **Midweek:** clear the Email Not Matched flags
- [ ] **Friday:** celebrate published entries in Discord
- [ ] **Monthly:** check in with each volunteer; review this guide for outdated info

#### Automations & integrations

| What | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Link submitter by email | Airtable automations (one per research table) | Credits each submission to the Active volunteer with a matching email, or ticks Email Not Matched |
| Discord notifications | Zapier (temporary) | Posts new submissions and sign-ups to the private staff channel |

**Planned for week 5:** built-in submission forms on the website, with Discord notifications sent directly from the web app. When that launches, turn off the Zaps, disconnect Zapier from Airtable and Discord, and remove the Zapier bot from the channel.

#### Access principles

- Give people **only the access their role needs**.
- Volunteer contact details stay in **Airtable**, never in this repository or in Discord messages.
- The Discord staff channel (where notifications go) stays **private**.
- Never share API tokens or webhook URLs in chat, notes, or code.

### Developer

Developers build the Open Access website: the public **Archive** and **Marketplace**, and the private **Volunteer Portal**.

#### The stack

| Layer | Tool |
|---|---|
| Framework | Next.js (React) with TypeScript |
| Styling | Tailwind CSS |
| Data | Airtable (one base: **Open Access**), temporary until we move to Postgres |
| Login | Supabase Auth (invite-only) |
| Hosting | Vercel |
| Notifications | Discord webhook (from the web app, week 5) |

#### What we're building

**Public**
- **Archive** (`/archive`): search and filter published research across all four tracks; card, timeline, map, and table views; detail pages; CSV/PDF export
- **Marketplace** (`/marketplace`): published Apprenticeship Opportunities with filters

**Private (login required)**
- **Volunteer Portal** (`/volunteers`): Research Tasks, Research Leads, and Resources, with Submit buttons
- **Week 5 upgrade:** built-in submission forms that save to Airtable, fill in the volunteer automatically from their login, and post to Discord

#### Rules

**Only show published records publicly.** The public site shows only records where **Task Status** is **Published**. Filter on the server, never in the browser.

**Keep secrets out of the code.**
- The Airtable token, Supabase keys, and Discord webhook URL live in `.env.local` locally and in **Vercel environment variables** in production.
- `.env.local` is in `.gitignore`. Never commit it.
- The public site uses a **read-only** Airtable token scoped to the Open Access base.

**Fetch data on the server.** Call Airtable only from server code (server components, route handlers, server actions). The token must never reach the browser.

**Protect volunteer data.** Never expose the **Volunteers** table, emails, or linked people fields on public pages.

#### Working with Claude Code

- Keep a `CLAUDE.md` in the repo root with the base ID, table names, the stack, and the rules above.
- Connect Airtable to Claude Code so it can read the live schema.

> [!CAUTION]
> I cannot repeat this enough: **work in branches and open pull requests. Don't push straight to `main`.**
